Title: Story Ending Check

Notes / Description:
Built a reusable story-ending continuity round. An author freezes required beats and a continuity boundary, submits a versioned ending, and GenLayer validators return a beat-closure mask plus COHERENT, OPEN_ENDED, or CONTRADICTORY. Exactly three on-chain reader votes determine whether a revision is invited, one revision is bounded, and the author alone adopts or declines. Literary quality and authorship are not automated.
